《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.7**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.7**.

**This version (v0.3.8)**

- New special event: **Volcano Sage** (Act 2, shared in multiplayer) with three options:
  humbly seek guidance (lose 8 HP, upgrade 1 card), debate him (256 gold, 1 Doubt),
  or "I already understand" (enter the special fight).
- New special monster: **Volcanic Beast** (event-only elite), 144/154 HP,
  starts with Burn Immunity and Fire Spit 3.
  Move loop 1→2→3: 3×4 + Fire Spit stacks of Burn + 1 Vulnerable / 24 damage /
  3 Burns into your discard pile + 4 Strength + 2 Fire Spit.
- The fight uses the Act 2 elite battle music and its own combat background.

60 cards / 26 powers / 9 relics / 3 potions / 2 special events / 1 special monster /
full dialogue for all 9 Ancients. Three spell schools: Fire, Lightning, Ice.
Chinese + English text.

Source: https://github.com/Traumere7/sts2mod-wizard
