《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.8**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

**本版（v0.5.7）：新事件「未知海洋」**

- **新事件「未知海洋」**（第三阶段，单人）：卡组里至少有 1 张可附魔的牌时才会出现。
  - **岸边休息**：获得遗物「水蓝之心」。
  - **纵身一跃**：将一张诅咒牌「梦魇」加入牌组，并为一张牌附魔「不朽」。
- **新遗物「水蓝之心」**（事件）：每当你打出一张带有**附魔**的卡牌，对所有敌人造成 3 点伤害。
- **新诅咒「梦魇」**：不可被打出；永恒。每当你抽到这张牌时，将此牌的一张复制品加入你的**弃牌堆**。
- **新附魔「不朽」**：这张牌失去「消耗」与「即毁」词条；如果它是能力牌，打出后不会消失，而是进入弃牌堆。

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.8**.

**This version (v0.5.7): new event "Unknown Sea"**

- **New event — Unknown Sea** (Act 3, single player): only appears when your deck has at
  least one enchantable card.
  - **Rest by the Shore**: obtain the relic **Aqua Heart**.
  - **Dive In**: add the curse **Nightmare** to your deck and enchant a card with **Immortal**.
- **New relic — Aqua Heart** (event): whenever you play an **Enchanted** card, deal 3 damage
  to ALL enemies.
- **New curse — Nightmare**: Unplayable, Eternal. Whenever you draw it, add a copy of it into
  your **Discard Pile**.
- **New enchantment — Immortal**: the card loses Exhaust and Obliterate; if it is a Power,
  it goes to your Discard Pile after being played instead of disappearing.

80 cards / 32 powers / 10 relics / 3 potions / 4 special events / 1 special monster /
full dialogue for all 9 Ancients.

Source: https://github.com/Traumere7/sts2mod-wizard
