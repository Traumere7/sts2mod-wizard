《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.8**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.8**.

**This version (v0.6.3): 5 new cards + Displacement + fixes**

- **New cards**
  - **Light the Lamp** — 1 · Common · Skill: Gain 6(8) Block. Transform a card in your Hand into a Spark.
  - **Calcination** — 1 · Common · Attack: Deal 8(11) Fire damage. Upgrade all cards in your Hand that were added to this combat.
  - **Fireworks** — 1 · Uncommon · Skill: Add 3(4) Spark cards to your Hand.
  - **Undying** — 1(0) · Uncommon · Skill: Next turn, gain Energy equal to the number of cards you created this turn.
    In combat the card face also shows "(You have created x cards this turn.)".
  - **Spark** — 0 · Token · Attack: Deal 2(4) Fire damage. Exhaust.
- **Balance**
  - **Displacement**: now removes **half** your Block (was all); ratio 4(3) → **3(2)** Block per Spell Power.
- **Other**
  - "Flame Spark" renamed to **Ember** (class, art, keys).
  - Fixed: cards produced by Transmutation (Transmogrify / Quickening / Animism) were **counted twice**
    (affected Catalyzation, Cauda Pavonis, Crystallization, Undying).
  - Fireworks no longer plays a card close-up when creating Sparks; Fireworks and Light the Lamp
    now show a Spark hover tip.

90 cards (84 in the draft pool) / 38 powers / 10 relics / 3 potions / 4 special events /
1 special monster / full dialogue for all 9 Ancients.
