《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.7**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.7**.

**This version (v0.5.3): Bug fixes**

- Fixed damage resolution when **Pyromaniac** and **Burning Soul** are both active.
  (Vanilla Vigor never clears its internal attack tracking, so if Vigor was topped up
  during an attack, every later attack card silently lost the bonus. Fixed.)
- Aether Arrow / Mana Burst got their hit sound back (Regent's guiding-star chime), and
  the aether star impact now has a white-and-gold variant.

75 cards / 29 powers / 9 relics / 3 potions / 3 special events / 1 special monster /
full dialogue for all 9 Ancients.
Text available in Simplified Chinese / Traditional Chinese / Japanese / English.

Source: https://github.com/Traumere7/sts2mod-wizard
