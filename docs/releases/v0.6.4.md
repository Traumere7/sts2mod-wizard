《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.8**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.8**.

**This version (v0.6.4): bug fixes**

- Fixed: White Night's Chill did not trigger Double Ricochet returning from your discard pile.
- Fixed: only the first hit of Double Ricochet benefited from Vigor.

90 cards (84 in the draft pool) / 38 powers / 10 relics / 3 potions / 4 special events /
1 special monster / full dialogue for all 9 Ancients.

Source: https://github.com/Traumere7/sts2mod-wizard
