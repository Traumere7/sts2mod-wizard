《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.8**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

  - **白夜 White Night** — 1 费 · 罕见 · 攻击：造成 11(14) 点寒冰伤害；如果敌人有燃烧，则给予 2(3) 层虚弱和 2(3) 层寒冷。

  - **灵光 Flicker** — 0 费 · 普通 · 攻击：造成 5(7) 点雷电伤害；你每生成 2 张卡牌，就把这张牌从抽牌堆或弃牌堆放入你的手牌。

  - **书签**：格挡 7(9) → 9(11)。
  - **炽焰术**：每段伤害 6(7) → 7(8)。
  - **天火浩荡**：费用 4 → 3；增加燃烧 10(12) → 7(9)；燃烧结算 1 次 → 2 次。
- **其他**
  - 灵光被效果放入手牌时不再播放卡牌特写演出。

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.8**.

**This version (v0.6.1): 5 new cards + balance**

- **New cards**
  - **White Night** — 1 · Uncommon · Attack: Deal 11(14) Ice damage. If the enemy has Burn, apply 2(3) Weak and 2(3) Chill.
  - **Electric Arc** — 1 · Uncommon · Attack: Apply 3(4) Current Overflow, then deal 7(9) Lightning damage.
  - **Kindling** — 1 · Rare · Power: Whenever you deal Fire damage, increase Burn on that enemy by 1(2).
  - **Wildfire** — 3 · Uncommon · Attack: Deal 5(7) Fire damage to ALL enemies 3 times. Whenever you Exhaust a card, this card costs 1 less until played.
  - **Flicker** — 0 · Common · Attack: Deal 5(7) Lightning damage. Whenever you create 2 cards, put this card from your Draw Pile or Discard Pile into your Hand.
- **Balance**
  - **Screen**: Block 5(7) → 7(10).
  - **Pyro Judgment**: initial Burn each turn 3(4) → 4(5).
  - **Bookmark**: Block 7(9) → 9(11).
  - **Blazing Flame**: damage per hit 6(7) → 7(8).
  - **Skyfire Rampage**: cost 4 → 3; Burn increase 10(12) → 7(9); Burn resolves 1 → 2 times.
- **Other**
  - Flicker no longer plays a card close-up effect when it returns to your hand.

85 cards / 37 powers / 10 relics / 3 potions / 4 special events / 1 special monster /
full dialogue for all 9 Ancients.
