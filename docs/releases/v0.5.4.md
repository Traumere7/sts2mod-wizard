《杀戮尖塔 2》巫师角色 mod —— 构建产物存档。

解压后把三个文件放进游戏目录的 `mods\Wizard\`：

```
Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\
├── Wizard.dll
├── Wizard.json
└── Wizard.pck
```

同时需要 `mods\BaseLib\`（创意工坊订阅即可，要求 **≥ 3.4.8**）。

> 推荐走创意工坊安装，会自动更新：
> https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3807136195

Wizard — a character mod for **Slay the Spire 2**.

Extract the three files into `Slay the Spire 2\mods\Wizard\`. Requires **BaseLib ≥ 3.4.8**.

**This version (v0.5.4): two new cards + compatibility with the Nov 13 update**

- **New card — Mercury** (1 · Uncommon · Power): the first card you create each turn is
  Enchanted with **Swift 2** (upgraded: the first two cards).
- **New card — Eternal Echo** (1 · Rare · Power): choose 1 card in your Hand and Enchant it with
  **Reprise** (Replay 2). **Obliterate**; upgrading grants Retain.
  - New enchantment **Reprise** — this card gains **Replay 2**.
  - New keyword **Obliterate** — after you play this card, remove it from your deck
    (not the same as Exhaust).
  - Enchanting a card **from your deck** is permanent; enchanting a **temporarily created**
    card only lasts for the current combat.
- Updated for the Nov 13 public-beta build (buildid 26625880); BaseLib requirement raised to **3.4.8**.
- Other:
  - Aether Arrow now has an upgrade: damage 9 → **12**.
  - Eternal Echo can no longer be generated during combat (same rule as Healing Spell / Asceticism).
  - Fixed Eternal Echo writing its enchantment back to the deck, which leaked Reprise onto the
    deck copy when a temporary card was enchanted.
  - Mercury's card face no longer explains its own buff; its text now reads "the first card /
    the first two cards" depending on upgrade.

77 cards / 30 powers / 9 relics / 3 potions / 3 special events / 1 special monster /
full dialogue for all 9 Ancients.
Text available in Simplified Chinese / English (the new enchantment and keyword strings are
Chinese/English only for now; Traditional Chinese and Japanese fall back to English).
